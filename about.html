About us

We think you learn best by actually making something.

BrushStroke started as an annoyance with courses that end in a quiz instead of a finished product. So we made the opposite.

MISSION

Start making.

Most course platforms measure success in videos watched. We measure it in pieces finished, a character, a paint study, an illustration. Every course leads to a finished piece, so you always know what you're working toward.

We keep modules short and checkpointed, so progress is visible from day one.

VALUES

What we optimise for.

  • Finish what you start Every course ends with a piece you can admire.
  • Let real artists teach. Instructors are current working professionals, NO A.I.
  • Respect your time. Short, checkpointed modules over long uninterrupted sessions.
