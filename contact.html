Help centre

Are the courses really free?

Yes. Every course on BrushStroke is free to enrol in. You only need an account so we can save your progress.

How do I get a certificate?

Tick every task in every lesson, then press Finish course on the last lesson. Your certificate appears on your profile.

Where can I find my courses?

Log in and choose My Courses in the top menu. Each course picks up at your next lesson.

Can I stay logged in?

Tick Remember me on the log in page and BrushStroke will keep you signed in on that device.

I forgot my password

Password reset isn't available in this student version yet. Send us a message and we'll help.
